<h1>“Does it dance? Does it catalyze a joyful affirmation of life?” — Friedrich Nietzsche</h1>
<br>
<br>
<p>I’m Phoemela and this is my home on the web where I share things that are important to me with whoever’s willing to visit. Like most people, I enjoy reading books and listening to <a href="https://radio4000.com/comballar" target="_blank">music</a>.</p>
<p>My family name, <i>Ballaran</i>, is the third-person plural future tense of the Catalan word <i>ballar</i> (to dance) and translates as “they will dance.” <i>Com ballar</i> means “how to dance.”</p>
<p>I sometimes broadcast my pressing thoughts on <a href="https://www.instagram.com/com.ballar/" target="_blank">Instagram</a>, but these days I mostly spend a portion of my leisure time on <a href="https://comballar.tumblr.com/" target="_blank">Tumblr</a> and <a href="https://www.are.na/phoemela-ballaran/" target="_blank">Are.na</a>.</p>
<p>Feel free to explore the space while I get you some tea—I’ll be right back.</p>
